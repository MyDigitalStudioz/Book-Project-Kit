## Changes from the Santa summary, and why
1. **License: CC BY → All Rights Reserved.** CC BY would let anyone legally republish and sell the book with only attribution — and it contradicts Harlow's own copyright page ("All rights reserved"). This is the one change that actually matters legally.
2. **Description: was empty — drafted, then SHORTENED on Lois's feedback (2026-11-04: "cute, but too long and overcomplicated")** to Jon's own cut, lightly cleaned. Short and sweet is the standing version now.
3. **Keywords: was empty — filled** with the terms a searching parent/grandparent would use.
4. **Audience: 2–12 → 3–8.** Harlow is a read-aloud picture book; 3–8 is the honest shelf. (The "fun adults" charm moved into the description's tone, where it belongs — Rob's "adults and kids would equally enjoy it" proved the point.)
5. **Title:** "Hardcover" kept in the internal project name only; the book's title is just "Harlow's Accidental Journey."
6. **Jon's contributor role named:** Illustrator (he also designed the book; the edition line inside already credits design).
